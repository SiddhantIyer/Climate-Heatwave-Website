What is a Heatwave?

A heatwave is a prolonged period of abnormally high temperatures that can severely impact human health, agriculture, ecosystems, and economic activities.

Why is Climate Intelligence Important?

Climate Intelligence combines Artificial Intelligence, historical weather data, and monitoring systems to predict future heatwaves and issue early warnings before extreme weather events occur.
