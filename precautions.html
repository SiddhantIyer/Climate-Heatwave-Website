Heatwave Safety Guidelines

Stay Safe. Stay Hydrated. Stay Prepared.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you are not thirsty. Avoid excessive tea, coffee, and alcohol.

👕 Wear Light Clothing

Choose light-coloured, loose-fitting cotton clothes to reduce heat absorption.

☀ Avoid Peak Sunlight

Avoid outdoor activities between 12 PM and 4 PM when temperatures are highest.

🏠 Stay Indoors

Remain in shaded or air-conditioned places whenever possible.

🍎 Eat Healthy

Consume fruits, vegetables, and ORS to maintain hydration and electrolyte balance.

🚑 Emergency

Seek medical attention immediately if someone experiences dizziness, fainting, confusion, or heat stroke.

Do's and Don'ts

✅ Do's

  • Drink water frequently.
  • Carry an umbrella or cap.
  • Wear sunscreen.
  • Take regular breaks while working outdoors.
  • Check weather updates.

❌ Don'ts

  • Do not stay in direct sunlight for long periods.
  • Do not leave children or pets inside parked vehicles.
  • Avoid heavy physical exercise during peak heat.
  • Avoid dehydration.
  • Avoid junk food and excessive caffeine.
